Usage rules

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space. Keep empty space around the logo of at least half the mark's diameter on every side. Nothing else enters this zone: no text, photos or edges.

Minimum size. Horizontal lockup 140 px wide on screen (35 mm in print). Mark alone 24 px on screen (10 mm in print). Below these sizes, use the app icon.

Colour

Palette and tokens

Forest green is the brand. Harvest gold is the spark. About 60% of any page should be light grounds, 25% forest green, 10% gold and 5% cocoa brown and leaf green. Tokens are in brand-tokens.css and brand-palette.json.

brand-forest-green

#0E4D2C

Primary brand colour. Logo disc, primary buttons, hero bands, footer. Pair with on-brand text.

brand-harvest-gold

#E2A529

Accent fill: key numbers, highlights, the logo's furrow arcs and leaf, secondary call-to-action fills with on-gold text. Never text on light surfaces (2.2:1).

brand-cocoa-brown

#5B3A24

Secondary brand colour for export, product and premium sections and the logo descriptor line. Text on surface-ivory passes at 9.2:1.

brand-leaf-green

#7DB33F (dark #9CCB63)

Supporting fill for sustainability content, the logo's right leaf, chart series and illustration. Decorative only on light surfaces (2.5:1 on white); never text there.

brand-savanna-ivory

#F8F4EA

Brand ivory: the warm off-white of print, section backgrounds and reversed logo marks.

brand-charcoal

#1F2622

Brand near-black for print body text and dark photography overlays.

surface

#FFFFFF (dark #0F1A14)

Default page background.

surface-ivory

#F8F4EA (dark #16261D)

Alternate section background and cards on surface. The brand's signature warm ground.

surface-sand

#EFE8D8 (dark #1E3127)

Raised or nested panels, table headers, stat strips on surface-ivory.

ink

#1F2622 (dark #F3EFE4)

Body and heading text on surface, surface-ivory and surface-sand (12.6:1 or more in both themes).

ink-muted

#56605A (dark #B5BDB7)

Secondary text, captions and metadata on surface, surface-ivory and surface-sand (5.3:1 or more light, 8.2:1 or more dark).

ink-brand

#0E4D2C (dark #9CCB63)

Links, eyebrow labels and accent headings on surface and surface-ivory (9.0:1 or more light, 8.4:1 or more dark).

ink-gold

#8A5A12 (dark #E2A529)

Gold-toned text on surface (5.9:1 light) and dark surfaces (7.3:1 or more). Use it where the design wants gold words; never brand-harvest-gold on light.

on-brand

#F8F4EA

Text and icons on surface-brand and brand-forest-green fills (9.0:1).

on-gold

#1F2622

Text and icons on brand-harvest-gold fills (7.1:1).
