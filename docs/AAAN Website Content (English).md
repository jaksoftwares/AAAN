## 3. About AAAN

**Hero**

- Headline: Advancing Africa's harvest since 2015.
- Sub-line: A pan-African agribusiness network, headquartered in Nairobi and working across Kenya, Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire.

**Our story**

- Title: It started with a question.
- Paragraph 1: In 2015, three graduates shared one frustration. Africa grows some of the world's most valuable crops, yet its farmers earn the least from them. Harvests were lost to poor seed and poor storage, and raw crops left the continent cheaply only to come back as expensive products.
- Paragraph 2: Aaron Appiagyei, an agricultural scientist, Margaret Paula, a food and nutrition specialist, and Antwi Kingsley, a technologist, asked a simple question: what if one network could fix every link, from the seedling to the shelf? They started in Ol Kalou, Nyandarua, with one nursery and 120 farmers. AAAN supplied clean seed potatoes and vegetable seedlings, trained the farmers in better methods, and promised to buy what they grew. Yields rose, losses fell, and incomes followed.
- Paragraph 3: The model worked, so we took it further. In 2017 we came to Ghana's cocoa and yam belt, and in 2020 to Côte d'Ivoire. We added processing, so that more of each harvest's value stays in Africa. In 2019 we moved our head office to Nairobi, closer to our buyers, partners and the region's trade routes.
- Paragraph 4: Today AAAN works with more than 12,000 farmers and supplies buyers in 15 countries. Our belief has not changed: **African agriculture, advanced, can feed Africa and the world.**

## 4. What we do

**Overview page**

- Headline: We work on every link of the chain.
- Intro: Most agricultural companies do one thing: grow, buy or trade. AAAN does all of it, from the seedling to the buyer, so that quality, traceability and value are in our hands at every step.
- Cards: Seedlings and nurseries · Farming and technology · Value addition and processing · Export and trade · Farmer network and training

### Seedlings and nurseries

- Headline: Every good harvest starts with a good seedling.
- The problem: Many African farmers plant saved or uncertified material that carries disease and gives low yields. No amount of hard work can make up for a poor start.
- How we do it:
  1. **Six nurseries** in Kenya, Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire raise planting material under controlled conditions.
  2. **Improved varieties:** hybrid cocoa, grafted cashew, clean cassava cuttings, tissue-culture bananas and plantains, certified seed potatoes and vegetable seedlings.
  3. **Certification:** our seed and plant material is inspected and certified by national seed authorities, including KEPHIS in Kenya.
  4. **Delivery and planting support:** seedlings reach farmers with planting guidance and a follow-up visit.
- Proof: 2.5 million+ seedlings a year · 15 million since 2015 · 2,000 tonnes of certified seed potatoes a year
- Call to action: Order seedlings for your farm or cooperative.

### Farming and technology

- Headline: Better methods. Bigger harvests.
- The problem: Smallholders often farm without soil data, timely advice or water control, so yields stay low and risk stays high.
- How we do it:
  1. **Soil testing** and precise fertiliser plans for each farm.
  2. **Water:** drip irrigation and greenhouses for vegetables in Kenya.
  3. **Integrated pest management** that cuts chemical use.
  4. **The AAAN farmer app:** weather alerts, crop advice and market prices on the farmer's phone.
  5. **GPS farm mapping** that proves where every crop was grown.
- Proof: 35% average yield increase for network farmers · 18,000+ hectares under AAAN-supported production
- Call to action: See how our technology traces your supply.

### Value addition and processing

- Headline: More of every harvest's value stays in Africa.
- The problem: Raw crops earn the lowest prices, and the jobs and margins of processing happen elsewhere.
- How we do it, in five facilities:
  - **San-Pédro, Côte d'Ivoire:** cocoa processing into cocoa liquor, butter and powder.
  - **Bouaké, Côte d'Ivoire:** cashew shelling, peeling and grading into export kernels.
  - **Kumasi, Ghana:** cassava into gari and high-quality cassava flour.
  - **Kinangop, Kenya:** milk chilling and processing into pasteurised milk, yoghurt and ghee.
  - **Nairobi, Kenya:** an export packhouse for fresh vegetables and fruit.
- Proof: 5 processing and packing facilities · HACCP-based food-safety systems in every plant
- Call to action: Explore our processed products.

### Export and trade

- Headline: Reliable supply to 15 countries.
- The problem: Buyers need consistent volumes, clear specifications and paperwork they can trust, every time.
- How we do it:
  1. **Sourcing from 17 sites** in three countries, so supply never depends on one harvest.
  2. **Specifications and samples** before every contract.
  3. **Export logistics** through Mombasa, Tema, Abidjan and San-Pédro ports and Nairobi's JKIA airport.
  4. **Full documentation:** phytosanitary certificates, certificates of origin, quality certificates and lot traceability.
- Proof: 30,000+ tonnes exported a year · 15 countries across Africa, Europe, the Middle East and North America
- Call to action: Request a quote.

### Farmer network and training

- Headline: Growing together.
- The problem: Farmers working alone have little access to inputs, knowledge or fair markets.
- How we do it:
  1. **Field schools and demonstration farms** at Ol Kalou, Kumasi and Bouaké.
  2. **Inputs on credit:** seedlings, fertiliser and tools, repaid at harvest.
  3. **A guaranteed buyer:** agreed prices and quality grades before planting.
  4. **Cooperative support:** organising, record-keeping and certification.
- Proof: 12,000+ farmers · 160+ cooperatives and groups · 28,000+ farmers trained · 42% women
- Call to action: Join the network.

### Cocoa

- Headline: Cocoa from the heart of the world's cocoa belt.
- Sub-line: Traceable, deforestation-free cocoa beans and cocoa products from Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire.
- At a glance: Hubs: Sefwi Wiawso and Kumasi (Ghana), San-Pédro and Soubré (Côte d'Ivoire) · Main crop: October to March; mid-crop: May to August · Markets: Europe, USA · Certifications: Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade, organic, EUDR-ready traceability
- From our farms: Our cocoa starts in our nurseries, with high-yield seedlings for farmers who are renewing old trees. Every partner farm is GPS-mapped, so we can show that no cocoa comes from cleared forest. Beans are fermented for six days under banana leaves, sun-dried on raised tables, and checked for moisture and bean count before they are bagged. In San-Pédro we process part of the harvest into cocoa liquor, butter and powder.
- Products:
  - **Cocoa beans:** Grade 1, fermented and sun-dried, moisture 7.5% or less, jute bags.
  - **Cocoa liquor:** 25 kg blocks.
  - **Natural cocoa butter:** 25 kg cartons.
  - **Cocoa powder:** natural and alkalised, 10 to 12% fat, 25 kg bags.
  - **Cocoa nibs:** roasted, vacuum-packed.
- Responsibility note: We do not allow child labour on any farm in our network. Every cooperative is monitored and supported to keep children in school.

### Cashew nuts

- Headline: Cashew, shelled and graded where it grows.
- Sub-line: Raw cashew nuts and premium kernels from Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana and the Kenyan coast.
- At a glance: Hubs: Korhogo and Bouaké (Côte d'Ivoire), Techiman (Ghana), Mombasa coast (Kenya) · Season: February to May in West Africa; September to January in Kenya · Markets: Europe, USA, Middle East · Certifications: Fairtrade, organic, HACCP-based food safety for kernels
- From our farms: Cashew farmers in our network collect nuts as they fall, dry them well and sell to us at agreed grades. Every lot is cut-tested for kernel outturn before purchase. Since 2026, our Bouaké plant shells, peels and grades kernels, so that more of cashew's value stays in Côte d'Ivoire.
- Products:
  - **Raw cashew nuts:** graded by kernel outturn, 80 kg jute bags.
  - **Cashew kernels:** W180, W240, W320 and W450, plus splits and pieces, in vacuum packs in 22.68 kg (50 lb) cartons.
  - **Roasted cashews:** salted and plain, retail and bulk.
  - **Cashew apple juice:** seasonal.

### Oil palm

- Headline: Palm oil, produced responsibly.
- Sub-line: Crude and refined palm oil and kernel products from Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire.
- At a glance: Hubs: Eastern Region (Ghana), Aboisso (Côte d'Ivoire) · Season: all year, with a peak from March to June · Markets: West Africa, Europe · Certifications: RSPO, EUDR-ready traceability
- From our farms: We work with smallholder oil palm growers on existing farmland, never on newly cleared forest. Fresh fruit bunches reach the mill within 24 hours of harvest, which keeps free fatty acids low and quality high.
- Products:
  - **Crude palm oil (CPO):** free fatty acids 5% or less, in flexitanks or drums.
  - **Refined palm olein:** for food manufacturing and retail.
  - **Palm kernel oil.**
  - **Palm kernel cake:** for animal feed.

### Cassava

- Headline: Cassava, from staple to stable income.
- Sub-line: Fresh cassava and processed cassava foods from Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire.
- At a glance: Hubs: Kumasi (Ghana), Bouaké (Côte d'Ivoire) · Season: all year · Markets: West Africa, plus African food stores in Europe and the USA · Standards: HACCP-based processing, Ghana FDA registered
- From our farms: Cassava feeds millions, but fresh roots spoil within two days of harvest. We give farmers clean, disease-free cuttings and buy their roots for processing the same day, turning a perishable crop into products with a long shelf life.
- Products:
  - **Gari:** white and yellow, fine and coarse, in 1 kg, 5 kg and 25 kg bags.
  - **High-quality cassava flour:** for bakeries and food manufacturers.
  - **Cassava starch:** food grade.
  - **Attieké:** fresh and dried, from Côte d'Ivoire.

### Yams

- Headline: West Africa's yams, for kitchens around the world.
- Sub-line: Export-grade yams and yam flour from Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire.
- At a glance: Hubs: Techiman (Ghana), Bouaké (Côte d'Ivoire) · Season: August to December, stored for supply all year · Markets: United Kingdom, Europe, USA · Certifications: GlobalG.A.P.
- From our farms: Our farmers grow yam from improved seed yams, staked and mulched for bigger, cleaner tubers. At our Techiman yard, each tuber is cleaned, graded, wrapped and packed for its journey.
- Products:
  - **Fresh yams:** white yam (Pona and Laribako) and water yam, 1.5 to 3 kg tubers, 25 kg cartons.
  - **Yam flour (elubo).**
  - **Yam chips:** dried.

## 8. Sustainability and impact; Quality and certifications

### Sustainability and impact

- Headline: Growing better. Feeding further.
- Intro: We measure our success by what changes on the farms we work with: more food, better incomes, healthier land.
- Impact figures: 12,000+ farmers · 42% women · 35% average yield increase · 420+ direct jobs and 2,000+ seasonal jobs · 55,000 tonnes of food a year

**Five impact themes**

1. **Farmers first.** When a farmer plants clean seed, learns better methods and knows who will buy the harvest, everything improves. Our network farmers' yields have risen by 35% on average.
2. **Food on African tables.** Most of what we handle feeds people in Kenya, Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire: potatoes, milk, maize, cassava, yams and vegetables. Drying, cold storage and processing mean less food is lost after harvest.
3. **Jobs where they are needed.** Our nurseries, plants and packhouses employ more than 420 people and offer over 2,000 seasonal jobs each year, most of them in rural areas and many held by women and young people.
4. **Value that stays in Africa.** Processing cocoa, cashew, cassava and milk in our own facilities creates skilled jobs and earns more per tonne for the region.
5. **Healthy land.** Organic methods, composting, integrated pest management, efficient irrigation, and cocoa and palm oil that never come from cleared forest.

**Responsible sourcing**

- No deforestation: every cocoa and oil palm farm in our network is GPS-mapped and checked against forest maps, in line with the EU Deforestation Regulation.
- No child labour: we monitor farmer groups, train communities, and support school attendance in cocoa and cashew areas.
- Fair dealing: prices and quality grades are agreed before planting, and payments are made on time, increasingly by mobile money.

**Our contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals:** Zero hunger (2) · Gender equality (5) · Decent work and economic growth (8) · Responsible consumption and production (12) · Climate action (13)

**Downloads:** Impact report · Sustainability policy · Responsible sourcing policy

### Quality and certifications

- Headline: Quality you can trace.
- Intro: Buyers trust AAAN because every product has a history we can show: where it was grown, how it was handled and how it was tested.

**Five quality checkpoints, from seed to shipment**

1. **Certified planting material** from our own nurseries.
